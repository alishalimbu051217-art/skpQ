Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

In short: we collect only what the app needs to work, we do not sell your data, and we never see your payment PIN or card details.

1. Who we are

SkpQ (Skip the Queue) is a college project that demonstrates a self-checkout app for stores. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google name, email address and profile photo.
  • Optional profile details: age, gender, phone number and a profile picture, only if you choose to add them in Edit Profile.
  • Order details: the items you scanned, quantities, prices, weights, totals, the checkout token, the order status and the time of the order.
  • Visit information: the time of your last visit, used to show Last Visit on your dashboard.
  • On your device: your current cart and your light or dark mode choice are saved in your browser's local storage.

3. Camera

The camera is used only to scan barcodes. Scanning is done on your device, and camera images are not uploaded or stored by SkpQ.

4. Payments

When you pay by UPI, your own UPI app handles the payment. SkpQ does not receive or store your UPI PIN, card number or bank login. We only record that an order was marked as paid.

5. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account working.
  • To build your bill, generate your token and let staff verify your order.
  • To show your profile, order history, total spent and loyalty points.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Who can see your information

  • Store staff can see your name and the contents of your order while verifying it.
  • The services that run the app can process the data for us: Google Firebase (sign-in and database) and the web host that serves the site.

7. How long we keep it

Your account and order history are kept while the app is in use. You can ask for your data to be deleted by contacting the SkpQ project team or the store where you use the app.

8. Your choices

  • Edit or remove your optional profile details from Edit Profile.
  • Change your password, or sign out, from the Profile page.
  • Clear the data saved on your device by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

9. Children

SkpQ is meant for general shoppers and is not designed for young children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as the project changes. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
