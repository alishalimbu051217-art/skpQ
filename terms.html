Please note: SkpQ is a college project and prototype. It is provided to demonstrate a self-checkout idea and is not a commercial product.

1. About SkpQ

SkpQ (Skip the Queue) lets shoppers scan the barcodes of items they want to buy using their phone camera, build a bill, generate a token or QR code, and have a store associate verify the basket before payment. By creating an account or signing in, you agree to these Terms.

2. Your account

  • You can sign in with Google or with an email address and password.
  • Give correct information and keep your password private. You are responsible for activity on your account.
  • You can update your details or sign out at any time from the Profile page.

3. Using the app properly

  • Scan only items that you actually intend to buy, and scan each item once for each unit you are taking.
  • Do not scan items you have not picked up, tamper with barcodes, or try to get around the weight check or the staff verification step.
  • Do not try to break, overload or gain unauthorised access to the app or its data.

4. Verification by store staff

Your order is not final until a store associate verifies it. The associate checks the items and the total weight of your basket against the expected weight. The associate may reject an order or ask you to rescan, and the store's decision is final.

5. Prices, weights and the final bill

Prices, item names and weights come from the store's product catalogue and may sometimes be wrong or out of date. The amount shown in the app is an estimate until the order is verified, and the store may correct it.

6. Payments

  • UPI payments open your own UPI app (for example Google Pay or PhonePe). SkpQ does not see or store your UPI PIN, card details or bank login.
  • In this version, you confirm inside the app after you have paid. Only confirm after the payment has actually gone through. Confirming a payment that was not made may be treated by the store as non-payment.
  • Card and wallet options shown in the app are placeholders and do not process real payments.

7. Camera and device permissions

The app asks to use your camera to scan barcodes. Scanning happens on your device. Images from the camera are not uploaded or saved by SkpQ.

8. Availability

The app needs an internet connection to sign you in and to link your phone with the staff screen. We do not promise that it will always be available or free of errors.
